Open Quest

An open quest registry on Arc. Finish three quests, earn a badge, or add a quest of your own.

What does it cost? Less than $0.05 in USDC covers everything. Each step has a network fee of about a quarter of a cent, and the 0.01 USDC you deposit comes back to you.
